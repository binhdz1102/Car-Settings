# Climate cabin top view

Create a 1600x900 layered vector top view of a generic vehicle cabin. The front of the vehicle
points upward. Required layers: `cabin_base`, `driver_zone`, `front_passenger_zone`, `rear_left_zone`,
`rear_center_zone`, `rear_right_zone`, `front_vents`, `rear_vents`, `defrost_windshield`, and
`recirculation_arrow`. Keep text and temperatures outside the artwork. The scene is context and
confirmed setting artwork; it is never live thermal telemetry.

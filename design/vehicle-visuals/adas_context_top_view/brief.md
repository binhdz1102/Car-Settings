# ADAS context top view

Create a 1600x900 layered vector road scene with an ego vehicle and neutral lane markings. Required
layers: `road`, `ego_vehicle`, `left_lane`, `right_lane`, `blind_spot_zone`, `cross_traffic_path`,
`target_vehicle`, and `warning_marker`. Keep blind spot and cross traffic as separate layers. Do not
draw live sensor cones, automatic braking, steering intervention, or collision guarantees.
